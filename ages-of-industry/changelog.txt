  Features:
    - Initial MVP release.
    - Added "Labor" fluid mechanic.
    - Added "Worker House" entity to produce Labor.
    - Added "Simple Meal" item (made from Wood) to fuel Workers.
    - Added "Wooden Pipe" for early game fluid transport.
    - Modified Stone Furnace to require Labor for operation.
    - Modified basic smelting recipes (Stone Brick, Iron Plate, Copper Plate) to require Labor.
    - Fixed recipe ingredient format to be compatible with Factorio 2.0 strict requirements.
    - Updated fluid box definitions to use 'volume' instead of 'base_area' for Factorio 2.0 compatibility.
